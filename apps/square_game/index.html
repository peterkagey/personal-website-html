Square Game

You can read about this app on my blog post My favorite sequences: "Not Equal" Grid.

Click here to learn how to play!
  • In this game, you will try a configuration of colors (i.e. numbers \(\{1,2\dots,n\}\)) on the square grid so that every color is adjacent to every other color. In particular, you want to do so using the fewest number of vertices possible.
  • Clicking or tapping on a vertex will decrease the label of the circle. The vertex will disappear if the label gets to zero.
  • As you play, you will notice that \(a(n) = k\) will update, where $n$ is the number of labels that are connected to everything smaller, and $k$ is the number of circles in your solution. (Your solution is a constructive upper bound for the sequence.)
  • Any red edges denote colors that are connected in multiple ways or are connected to themselves.
  • The "new" button will allow you to start a new game.
  • The save button will allow you to save your solution, which will automatically update the high-scores table.
  • On desktop, you can additionally:
    • Right click on a circle to increase its value.
    • Click and drag to move vertices.
    • Hit "backspace" while mousing over a vertex to delete it.
