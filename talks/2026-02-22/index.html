Triakis Icosahedron

Up to rotation and reflection, there are \(10\) polyforms with \(6\) faces on the triakis icosahedron, which has \(10 \times 6 = 60\) isosceles triangular faces.

Tetrakis Hexahedron

Up to rotation and reflection, there are \(6\) polyforms with \(4\) faces on the tetrakis hexahedron, which has \(6 \times 4 = 24\) isosceles triangular faces.

Triakis Octahedron

Up to rotation (but not reflection), there are two \(2\)-forms and five \(4\)-forms on the faces of the triakis octahedron, which has \({\color{#F8F}2 \times 2} + {\color{#FB8}5 \times 4} = 24\) isosceles triangular faces.

Rhombic Dodecahedron

Up to rotation and reflection, there is one \(1\)-form, one \(2\)-form, and three \(3\)-forms on the faces of the rhombic dodecahedron, which has \({\color{#F88}1} + {\color{#88F}2} + {\color{#8F8}3 \times 3} = 12\) rhombic faces.
