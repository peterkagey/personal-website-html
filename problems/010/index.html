Consider Ron Graham's sequence for LCM: look at sequences such that n=b1<b2<⋯<bt=k and LCM(b1,…,bt) is a perfect square.

Question

Let A300516(n) be the least k (as a function of n) such that such a sequence exists. What is A300516(n)?

Related questions

  1. For which values of n is A300516(n) nonsquare?
  2. For which values of n does the corresponding sequence have three or more terms?
  3. What is the analogous sequence for perfect cubes (or higher powers)?
