Open Problem Collection

In 2017, at the beginning of my time in graduate school, I started putting together a collection of problems that I thought were interesting. The collection now contains over 130 problems, which you can download as PDF or view in your browser.

A small subset of these problems have been adapted for the web, which you can find here.
