OEIS sequence A261865 describes "a(n) is the least k∈N such that some multiple of k lies in (n,n+1)." Clearly the asymptotic density of 2 in the sequence is 1/2.

Question

Let Sα⊂N denote the squarefree integers strictly less than α. Is the asymptotic density of squarefree j given by 1j∏s∈Sj(1−1s)?

Related questions

  1. Is there an algorithm to construct a value of n such that a(n)>K for any specified K? (Perhaps using best Diophantine approximations or something?)
  2. What is the asymptotic growth of the records?
  3. Given some α, what is the expected value of the smallest n such that Sα⊂{a(1),…,a(n)}?
  4. This sequence uses the "base sequence" {1,2,3,…}. On what other base sequences is this construction interesting?
  5. What is the smallest m∈N such that k21/m∈(n,n+1) for some k∈N?
  6. What is the smallest k∈N such that k21/m∈(n,n+1) for some m∈N?
