← Problem 125 →

Let G be a finite group, and call a sequence {gi∈G}i=1|G|−1 a Hamiltonian walk if for each g∈G there exists some n≥0 such that the n-th partial product pn=g, where pn=g1⋅g2⋯gn.

Question

Does every finite group have a Hamiltonian walk that is a palindrome?

Related questions

  1. If not, does every finite group have a Hamiltonian walk whose reversal is also a Hamiltonian walk?
  2. Is there an efficient way to compute how many essentially different Hamiltonian walks G has?
  3. For a group G, what proportion of Hamiltonian walks are reversible?
  4. Does every finite semigroup have a reversible Hamiltonian walk?
