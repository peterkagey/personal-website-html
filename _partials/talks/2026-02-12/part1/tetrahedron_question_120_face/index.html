How many ways are there to tile the tetrahedron using these four tile designs such that the tetrahedron looks the same after being rotated \(\pm120^\circ\) about a face (equivalently vertex)?
(There are \(8\) inequivalent ways in which we can do this.)
There is just \(1\) choice of tile for the rotating face.
Then we can choose any of the four tile designs for any of the remaining faces which then determines the other two.
Thus there are \(1 \times 4 = 4\) ways to tile the tetrahedron that look the same after \(120^\circ\) rotation of a face.
