My favorite shape is the pentagonal icositetrahedron. How many ways can tile the faces with \(3\)-colors up to its \(24\) symmetries?
A good guess is \[ \frac{3^{24}}{24} \approx 11\,767\,897\,353.4 \] using the heuristic "most colorings have no symmetry."
The correct answer is \[ A378475(3) = 11\,768\,099\,013, \] where A378475 is a sequence we added to the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences (OEIS).
