How many ways are there to tile the tetrahedron such that the tetrahedron looks the same after being rotated \(180^\circ\) along an edge?
(There are \(3\) inequivalent ways in which we can do this.)
We can choose a face and tile it any of the \(4\) ways.
This determines how we much tile the other face in its orbit.
Next we can choose either of the remaining faces and tile it any of the \(4\) ways, which determines the tiling on the fourth face.
Thus there are \(4 \times 4 = 16\) ways to tile the tetrahedron that look the same after \(180^\circ\) rotation.
