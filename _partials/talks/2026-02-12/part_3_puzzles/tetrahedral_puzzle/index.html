There are 11 tetrahedral-octahedral 4-celled polyforms up to rotation (but not reflection). In total, these have 20 octahedral cells and 24 tetrahedral cells.

Can these fit into the 5x scaled tetrahedron, which can be partitioned into 20 octahedra and 35 tetrahedra?

Andrew Kepert points out by taking the length-5 tetrahedron and removing the 4 tetrahedra at each vertex, the 6 tetrahedra at the center of each edge, and the tetrahedron in the center results in a region with full tetrahedral symmetry and which has 20 octahedral cells and 24 tetrahedral cells. Can the 11 polyforms pack this region?
