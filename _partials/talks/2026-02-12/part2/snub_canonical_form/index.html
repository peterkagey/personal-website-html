Next, we need to be able to determine when two polyforms are equivalent up to automorphisms. We do this by giving a canonical name for each orbit.
We start by shifting the polyform so that all coordinates are non-negative, and as small as possible.
Next, we move our piece into all possible orientations, which are specified by coset representatives of the quotient group of the Euclidean automorphism group by its translation subgroup.
The canonical representative for a polyform (orbit of an induced subgraph) is the lexicographically earliest vertex set with non-negative coordinates.
