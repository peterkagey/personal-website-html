We start by describing the Euclidean automorphisms, and a translation basis. \[\begin{align*} { \color{#FFA500} \vec{b}_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ \sqrt3 \end{pmatrix} } \qquad { \color{#D555D5} \vec{b}_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 3\sqrt3 \end{pmatrix} } \end{align*}\] All vertices have rational coordinates with respect to this basis \(\mathcal{B} = \{\vec{b}_1, \vec{b}_2\}\).
The automorphism group is generated by \[ \left\langle \left[ \begin{array}{rr|r} 1 & 0 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 0\\\hline 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right], \left[ \begin{array}{rr|r} 1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 1\\\hline 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right], \left[ \begin{array}{rr|r} 1 & 1 & 0\\ -1 & 0 & 0\\\hline 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right] \right\rangle, \] where the first two matrices generate the translation subgroup, and matrices act on projective coordinates.
