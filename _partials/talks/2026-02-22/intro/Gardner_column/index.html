In the December 1957, "Mathematical Games" column, Martin Gardner popularized a series of pentominoes packing puzzles, which he attributed to Sol Golomb (USC).
"It is [...] possible to fit the 12 pentominoes to form rectangles that are 6 × 10, 5 × 12, 4 × 15 and 3 × 20. The first three are shown on [the right]. The 3 × 20 rectangle, by all odds the most difficult, is left for the interested reader to construct."
—Martin Gardner, December 1957
