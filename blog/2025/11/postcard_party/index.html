Plot party postcard exchange

by Peter Kagey (2025-11-04)

In September, Michelle Chandra of Dirt Alley Design organized a #plot-party, where the participants exchanged postcards made on a pen plotter. I based my design on my "icosahedral symmetry" app, which allows the user to visualize certain nested shapes with 120 symmetries. As I wrote on the back:

The front of the postcard depicts a projection of three shapes: an icosahedron, a dodecahedron, and an icosidodecahedron scaled and aligned in such a way that the "convex hull" give a 120-sided shape called the "disdyakis triacontahedron."

Each of the 15 postcards contains a different perspective on these nested shapes.
