G4G16 Gift Exchange

by Peter Kagey (2026-02-22)

I participated in the Gathering 4 Gardner (G4G16) gift exchange, where I made 170 cut-and-fold versions of my pentagonal icositetrahedron puzzle. I talked about this puzzle in my talk, which you can see here.

Did you solve the puzzle, or are you interested? Email me at pkagey@cpp.edu or send me a message @peterkagey.com on Bluesky and let me know—I'll send you a satisfying 3D printed version of the puzzle for you to fiddle with!
