RSS Feeds

by Peter Kagey (2026-05-12)

Years ago, I pivoted away from getting news from social media and toward visiting dozens of newspaper and magazine homepages via a bookmark. Recently, I've made another shift toward using RSS, and embracing open protocols and the IndieWeb.

I use an RSS app called Feeder, which I chose more or less arbitrarily. It has a free tier, which comes with a Chromium addon and an iPhone app which sync up, and which I find useful.

In addition to my RSS feed for this site and the feeds I have built which allow you to get RSS for the OEIS, I also have a list of more traditional media outlets to follow, which I suspect say something about me!

When getting started, I found it tedious to build my collection of RSS feeds for the newspapers and magazines that I care about, so I wanted to share my own list below, which typically results in hundreds of new stories each day. (I manage these by saving the most interesting articles to my bookmark manager, Raindrop.io.)
